Flow-of-Funds Tracer

Follow victim funds from Tx0 through a network of subject wallets. Each withdrawal can be traced into a new wallet; the victim allocation carried by that withdrawal — under the selected accounting method — propagates automatically.
Accounting method
One method governs the entire trace. Switching it recomputes every wallet and every hand-off live. LIBR is shown per wallet as a ceiling only — it does not allocate per withdrawal, so it cannot propagate.

Whole trace computed client-side · no data leaves this page · method choice is an investigative & legal judgment, documented and defended.
Convention: when a mixed hand-off (part victim, part clean) enters a child wallet, FIFO/LIFO treat it internally as a victim lot followed by a clean lot; the ordering matters only when a withdrawal boundary splits that deposit. Linked deposits default their victim portion to the parent withdrawal's computed allocation (capped at the amount received); edited figures are flagged as analyst overrides and appear as such in the export.
